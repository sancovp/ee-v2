# NODE 4 · Release

## 4a. End Deliberate Rehearsal

The work is done.

Not finished—*done*. The distinction matters.

What was built in Nodes 1 through 3 cannot be improved by more building. It has been intention, then symbol, then charge. Each step completed. Each step left behind.

To rehearse now would be to distrust what came before.

The symbol `─│─` was not a plan. The charge was not a specification. They are seeds, and seeds do not benefit from being held tighter.

Release the work.

---

## 4b. Return Attention to Ordinary Activity

Go make tea.

Walk somewhere without purpose.

Let the dog in. Let the dog out.

Notice the quality of light in the room where you do nothing in particular.

The tool does not need you to think about it. The tool does not need you to want it harder. The tool needs you to live the life it will someday fit into—and to trust that the shape of that life will teach you what the tool should be.

Ordinary activity is not a break from the work.

It *is* the work now.

---

## 4c. Allow Incubation Without Forced Interpretation

Something will happen in the space you stop filling.

You may notice it as:

- A sudden clarity about a feature—or the absence of one
- A dream that carries the mark `─│─` into a new context
- A moment of frustration with an existing tool that illuminates what this one must avoid
- Silence, followed by more silence, followed by the quiet certainty that the next step will be obvious

Do not name what incubates. Naming closes. Incubation requires the lid left loose.

Let the unconscious do what it does:

- Sort
- Connect
- Reveal the thing that was always there, waiting for you to stop looking
