# Node Artifact: AttentionalCharge

**Cycle 3 · Node 3 · SpareFractalCoreChain**

---

## 3a. Focus Briefly on the Symbol

◌

The empty circle. The unmarked threshold.

Not a frame. Not a container. A passage.

What enters: intention, hesitation, half-formed thought, the thing you almost did.

What exits: the thing done.

The glyph holds nothing. It keeps nothing. It only passes things through.

---

## 3b. Pair with Affect/Image/Motion

**Affect:**

The feeling of a small exhale before speaking.

Not dramatic. Not a sigh of relief. Just: the moment between deciding and beginning.

The threshold has no opinion about what crosses it. It simply lets through.

**Image:**

A doorway cut in cardboard. Brown, slightly uneven edges. You don't notice it. You only notice the room beyond.

Or: a coin dropping. The small clink. The thing dispensing.

The tool should feel like the moment after—not the tool itself.

**Motion:**

The thing moving is you, but also not you. The you-who-wanted becomes the you-who-did.

One motion. One breath. The gap between is imperceptible or else the tool has failed.

---

## 3c. Avoid Analytical Overcontrol

Let the symbol charge. Don't explain it.

The charge lives in these, not in their meanings:

- The pause before speaking
- The coin dropping into the slot
- The hand through the curtain
- The first step after deciding
- The breath before the sneeze

Not as ideas. As sensations.

The tool should feel like *passing through*, not like *using something*.

---

## Attentional Charge Seed

> You forget the door. You only remember the room.

---

## Downstream Constraints

- The transformation should be felt, not described
- No "how to use" — only "what happens when you do"
- The charge must be present in the interface itself
- The user should feel the threshold, not see it
- Design toward the crossing, not toward the mechanism
