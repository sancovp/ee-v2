# NODE 3.AttentionalCharge — Artifact

## Step 3a. Focus Briefly on the Symbol

**The glyph:** `○──┄──○`

Two moments. A gap. No instruction.

The broken line between them is not absence — it is *pending*. The thing does not disappear; it is in transit. The circles are hollow because the thing never lives inside the tool. It moves through.

The symbol does not depict a container. It depicts a breath.

---

## Step 3b. Pair with Affect, Image, Motion

### Affect

The feeling of **setting something down without letting go**.

Not the relief of forgetting. The relief of *trusting* — that the thing will be there without you holding it. A stranger holding your bags while you run to catch something. You don't watch them. You trust.

The user is not organized. They are **unburdened**.

---

### Image

A single sheet of paper, folded once, sliding under a door. You hear it land on the other side. You don't need to see it. Tomorrow, on the other side of another door, it will be waiting.

Or: a coat left at a restaurant. You don't carry it all night. You trust the coat check. When you return, it is there.

The tool is the coat check. Not the coat rack. Not the closet. **The gesture of leaving something with someone who will keep it.**

---

### Motion

Three motions the tool must perform:

1. **In** — a single gesture. One tap. One drop. No categories. No tags. The motion is smaller than the thought. "I don't want to forget this" → the thing is gone from your head before you finish the sentence.

2. **Wait** — the tool does nothing. It holds. It does not remind, notify, or surface. The gap is the feature.

3. **Out** — when you return to the context, the thing is there. Not pushed. Not surfaced. You simply look for it and find it. The retrieval motion mirrors the deposit: one tap. One breath.

The entire tool fits in three gestures: **drop. wait. find.**

---

## Step 3c. Avoid Analytical Overcontrol

Do not design the tool.

Design the *moment* the tool enters.

A person is mid-task. Something occurs to them. They have 3 seconds before the thought dissolves. The tool must accept the thought in 2 seconds or less, with zero cognitive overhead.

The symbol already knows this. The gap is the silence between "I need this later" and "here it is." The tool must honor that silence — not fill it.

---

## AttentionalCharge Artifact

**The charged symbol:** `○──┄──○`

**What it carries:**

- **Affect:** Unburdening without forgetting
- **Image:** A coat checked, a note slid under a door
- **Motion:** Drop. Wait. Find.

**What it refuses:**

- Notification
- Organization
- Categories
- Reminders
- Any motion larger than a breath

**The minimum interface question answered:**

The interface must be smaller than the decision it holds. One field. One button. One place to look. The tool does not ask what you saved or why. It only returns what you left.

---

## Downward Carried Seed (for Node 4)

The charged symbol points toward a single constraint:

> *Build only the gesture. Nothing else survives.*

If the tool does not fit in a single breath of interaction — if it requires the user to think, choose, organize, or remember how to use it — it has betrayed the symbol.

The next node must answer: **What does "build only the gesture" actually mean in code?**
