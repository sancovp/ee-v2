# NODE 1.IntentSeed — Artifact

## Concrete Desire

A tool that saves one decision per day for someone who doesn't have time to think.

## Semantic Kernel

*Relief from micro-decision fatigue.*

Not productivity. Not optimization. **The quiet relief of not having to remember something small.**

## Isolated Artifact

```
Name: DropHold

Function: A single place to park decisions you don't want to make right now — 
          but will need later.

Input: "what do I want for lunch tomorrow"
Output: A held note. Gone from your head. Available when needed.

Use case: You think of something mid-day. You drop it. You forget it. 
          Tomorrow, it surfaces at the right moment.

Emotional signature: Light. Quiet. "I don't have to hold this."
```

## NewDesire Seed (carried forward)

*Who is the person this serves? What single context do they inhabit daily?*
