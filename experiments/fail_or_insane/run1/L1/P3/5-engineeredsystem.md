## Appendix B: Critical Recipes

### Lemon Caper Sauce

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups white wine
- 1 cup capers, drained
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 cups heavy cream
- Salt, white pepper

**Method:**
1. Reduce wine by half
2. Add capers, simmer 2 min
3. Add cream, reduce by third
4. Mount with butter
5. Season to taste
6. **Hold at 140°F+**

### Herb Butter

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb butter, softened
- 2 tbsp fresh herbs (chives, parsley, tarragon)
- Salt

**Method:**
1. Blend herbs into softened butter
2. Roll into medallions
3. Refrigerate until service
4. Place on grilled items for rest
